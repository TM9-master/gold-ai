Gold Trade AI — Privacy Policy

Application: Gold Trade AI (com.goldtradeai.app)
Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Effective: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Gold Trade AI mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and what control you have over it. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The short version. Gold Trade AI has no sign-up screen. We never ask for your name, your email address or a password. Your account is created automatically on your device from an Android system identifier. The only personal content you send us is the chart images you choose to analyse.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account identifier — no sign-up required

The App does not ask you to register. When you first open it, it reads the Android SSAID (a per-app, per-device identifier provided by the operating system) and uses it to create an account on our servers. From that identifier the App generates, on your device, a placeholder display name and a synthetic address of the form gold.<random-hash>@…. That address is an internal account key. It is not a real mailbox, it is not derived from any email address of yours, and we cannot use it to contact you.

We therefore do not collect your name, your real email address, or any password.

1.2 Content you choose to send

  • Chart images you upload from your gallery or capture with your camera for analysis.
  • Chart selections — the trading pair, timeframe and signal type you pick before requesting an analysis.
  • Analysis history — the signals generated for you and any tracking status you record against them.
  • Referral code — a code is generated for your account, and you may enter someone else's.
  • Support messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.

1.3 Collected automatically

  • Push notification token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging, so we can send you signal alerts. Only the token is transmitted.
  • Server log data — IP address, request timestamps and basic request metadata, recorded by our servers as a normal part of operating a service and used for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
  • Subscription status — if you purchase a subscription, Google Play provides us with a purchase token and its validation result so we can grant access.
  • Advertising data — see section 6.

1.4 What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address or password.
  • Precise or GPS location.
  • Contacts, SMS messages, call logs or microphone audio.
  • Photos other than the chart images you explicitly choose to analyse.
  • Your device model or operating-system details (the App does not transmit these, although the Google advertising SDK collects its own device signals — section 6).

3. Chart images and AI analysis

When you request an analysis, the chart image is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our servers and passed to a third-party AI vision model operated by Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service, which returns the written analysis. Processing happens server-side; the App never holds an AI provider key.

Please do not upload images containing personal information, account numbers, broker balances or anything else you would not want processed by a third-party AI service. Crop your screenshot to the chart itself.

Images and their generated analyses are retained so your signal history works. You can delete them at any time (section 8).

4. Not financial or investment advice

Gold Trade AI is an educational and analytical tool. Its output is not financial, investment or trading advice, is not a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument, and is not a personal recommendation of any kind. We are not a broker, we do not execute trades and we do not hold client funds.

Trading foreign exchange, commodities and derivatives carries a high level of risk and you can lose more than your initial deposit. Past performance does not indicate future results. You are solely responsible for your own trading decisions.

6. Advertising

Free-tier users see banner, interstitial and rewarded ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device's Advertising ID and collect information about ads you see and interact with in order to serve and measure advertising. This data is shared with Google as an independent controller.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID on Android at Settings → Privacy → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there. Subscribing to Pro removes ads from the App.

7. Data storage, security and transfers

Data is stored on our servers and transmitted over HTTPS. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our servers and our service providers may be located outside your country, including in jurisdictions whose data-protection laws differ from your own. By using the App you consent to that transfer.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. Server logs are kept for a limited period for security and debugging.

8.1 Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the App:

Settings → Delete Account

This permanently removes your account, your analysis history and your uploaded chart images. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel that separately in the Play Store.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@masterai.app from a device running the App, or by visiting our account deletion page. We respond within 30 days.

Note on device-bound accounts. Because your account is tied to your device identifier, factory-resetting your device or clearing the App's data may make your existing account unreachable and create a new one. We cannot recover an account whose identifier has changed, because we hold no email address or password for you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. Exercise any of these by contacting us at support@masterai.app.

9.1 EU / UK residents (GDPR)

Our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing analyses and subscriptions), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, service improvement) and consent (personalised advertising and push notifications, which you may withdraw at any time). You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

9.2 California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing". We do not sell personal information. Sharing an Advertising ID with Google for advertising may constitute "sharing" under the CPRA; you may opt out by disabling ad personalisation on your device or by subscribing to Pro. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

10. Children's privacy

Gold Trade AI is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are offered through Google Play. Payment is charged to your Google Play account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store under Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the App. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
